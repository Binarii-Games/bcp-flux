# Code of Conduct

## The standard

This project is harassment-free for everyone. One bar, applied the same way to
everyone, without exception.

Beyond that: technical disagreement is expected, bluntness is fine, and being
wrong is fine. Nobody is owed agreement or praise. What is owed is basic
respect, and that is where the obligation ends.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, including continuing after being asked to stop.
- Attacking a person instead of their code or their argument.
- Threats or intimidation, or encouraging anyone else to make them.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberate disruption: derailing discussions, spam, brigading.
- Sexual attention or advances.

Hard criticism of work is not harassment. "This is broken, and here is why" is
the job. "You are an idiot" is not.

## Scope

Applies anywhere the project lives (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages) and to anyone representing the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report to social@binariigames.com. Reports are handled privately, and the
reporter's identity is not shared without their agreement.

Responses scale with the behaviour and start with a warning. Repeated or
serious conduct ends in a ban from the project's spaces. The maintainers decide,
and the decision is final.
